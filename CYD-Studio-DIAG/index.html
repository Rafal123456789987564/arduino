GROVE HY2.0-4P · VIRTUAL PORT MAPPER
Port A (red) is normally I²C; B (black) is GPIO/ADC; C (blue) is UART. CYD has no built-in Grove sockets: connect through an appropriate adapter. Verify the actual PCB revision and voltage levels.
GND5V*IO1IO2

* 5V is a connector supply specification, NOT a 5V-tolerant ESP32 GPIO. Never connect automotive 12/24 V to GPIO. For UART and CAN/LIN use suitable transceivers.
